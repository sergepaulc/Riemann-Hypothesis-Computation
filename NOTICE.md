# Third-party notices and data

The existing MIT license applies to original code and documentation. It does not grant rights to third-party publications or source data.

The original zeta-zero tables are published by Andrew M. Odlyzko. They are not bundled as raw downloads. Their authoritative URLs and recorded fingerprints are in `data/odlyzko_tables.json`; the optional downloader retrieves and verifies them. Derived unfolded coordinates and numerical outputs needed for the main reruns are supplied.

Please cite the original data source as well as the paper and software.
