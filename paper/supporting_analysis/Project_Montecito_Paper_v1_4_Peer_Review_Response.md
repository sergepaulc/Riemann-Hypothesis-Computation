# Project Montecito Paper Draft 1.4 — Response to Draft 1.2 Peer Review

## Disposition

All Tier 1 and Tier 2 findings were accepted. The manuscript's scientific claims are unchanged except that the former statement that the \(10^{12}\) sample was “marginally high” has been withdrawn as insufficiently robust.

## Tier 1

1. **Finite-CUE \(\mu_2\) benchmark.** Added an analytic core-plus-halo expectation and an Experiment 1 histogram reconstruction. The truncated-sine value is \(1.332928978\), while the observed 64-realization CUE mean is \(1.326307870\), a \(-2.39\)-SE shortfall not explained by truncation or the finite-\(N\) pair-density correction. The paper now reports the discrepancy and does not use it to support a separate high-height zeta claim.
2. **Experiment 1 / Experiment 3 compatibility.** The full-sample reconstruction places \(Z_{12}\) slightly below and \(Z_{21}\) slightly above the truncated-sine value, while the 16-core means reverse their order. The paper attributes the earlier sign tension to finite calibration and sampling geometry and retains only the stable high-height conclusion.

## Tier 2

3. Replaced the previous mean-scale score by \(s_Y\sqrt{1/16+1/64}\), which includes uncertainty in the 64-window CUE reference mean.
4. Relabeled \(0.042085\%\) as the relative discrepancy in \(\|C\|_F^2\) (equivalently \(1+D_C\)); the relative discrepancy in \(D_C\) is also stated.
5. Defined \(\ell=\log(T/2\pi)\) and labeled the sinc/flat-window connection as a heuristic idealization rather than an identification with the Weil form.
6. Specified the CUE and Poisson dimensions, rotation, circumference scaling, core indices, and linear-difference convention.
7. Reworded the three-atom distribution as corresponding to the Alpöge--Furman extremal configuration.
8. Added the AI provenance of the 2026 theorem and a separate disclosure of AI use in Project Montecito.
9. Added acknowledgments and defined the scope of “independent review.”

The two \(S_{30}\) roundings were unified; \(D_C\) values were printed to sufficient precision; PRZZ2020 was corrected to “Paper No. 2”; the Odlyzko URL and access date were added; the special nature of the homometric \(\mu_3\) equality was noted; “first Hermitian instrument” was lowercased; all figure placeholders were replaced; the bandwidth-one ceiling and \(R(\psi_0)=4/3\) were checked against the cited source; and MSC 15A18 was added.
